## Nova Release

Nova was updated on October 8, 2026 to version 1.1.0

Release Notes:
* MGF reading implemented, and mzML and mzXML files larger than 2 GB are now readable.
* Nova targets .NET Standard 2.0, serving .NET Framework 4.8 and .NET 8 or later from one build.
* Several breaking changes. See the news post before upgrading.
